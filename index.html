THE KEEPER’S PRIMER

How to keep a graveyard.

Walk the grounds

WASD or arrows move. Shift dashes through danger. M opens the parish map.

Keep the peace

Space, J or left-click swings your blade. Time it well; every enemy telegraphs.

Tend the graves

E digs, buries, chops, mines, lights and speaks. Hold it for longer tasks.

Read the omens

Golden wisps mark restless graves. Teal light marks the Keeper’s Stone. The compass at the screen edge points to your next duty.

THE LOOP

Dig up remains → bury them in empty plots → earn coins and materials → hone your blade at the Keeper’s Stone → break the church seal.
